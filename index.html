Web application / OSINT platform

Heimdall

An interactive, near-real-time map of airspace threats that brings together more than two dozen selected monitoring channels and the official alert data. Unofficial: from channel posts, not radar.

How it works · Key advantages

How it works

The application automatically processes the stream of live updates, filters out the noise and presents one clear picture of the situation overhead — with a lightweight interface. While a threat is active, it refreshes every few seconds.

Key advantages

  • Cross-checked. Reports of the same target from different channels merge into one mark, and every mark shows the post it was read from. Alert colours come only from official data.
  • Zero tracking, privacy first. No registration, no accounts. In the app your places stay on your phone; the server keeps no IP addresses, no location and no browsing history — only an anonymous daily count. Location and notifications are optional; for notifications the server knows only a square of about 10 km.
  • Cross-platform. Runs in the browser on phones and PCs — nothing to install.

It does not replace the official alert and the sirens.
